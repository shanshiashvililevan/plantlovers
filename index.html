Recorded Works

Plant Music

PLANTLOVERS is an evolving artistic and musical library of plant bioelectric signals, gathered through recordings, field explorations, workshops and site-specific projects across different parts of the world.

Each release documents a particular encounter with plants, places and people. Measured signals are mapped into sound through bioelectric sensing and modular synthesis, then shaped through artistic interpretation.

PLANTLOVERS Vol. 1

Georgia · Thailand

The first experiments with maidenhair fern and ficus, recorded across Georgian and Thai ecosystems. Plant bioelectric activity becomes the control source for modular ambient compositions.
