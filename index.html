01 / TALK + LIVE DEMONSTRATIONListening to PlantsApprox. 2 hours · Includes a 15-minute live performance+

An accessible introduction to the electrical activity of plants and how it can become material for sound. Through examples and a live demonstration, participants follow the journey from sensing a living plant to translating changing signals into electronic sound.

What we explore

  • What plant bioelectric signals are — and what they are not
  • How sensors capture changes and how data is mapped to sound
  • How artistic decisions shape the listening experience
  • A short live plant-signal performance and audience Q&A

What participants take away

  • A clear understanding of the basic sensing-to-sound process
  • The ability to distinguish measured signals from musical interpretation
  • Ideas for applying attentive listening and creative technology in their own context

Suitable for schools, universities, cultural venues and festivals. No technical or programming background required.

02 / HANDS-ON WORKSHOPPlant Signals to Sound2-day intensive or 5-day programme+

Participants work directly with plant-signal sensing devices and electronic sound tools. The sessions move from observation and connection to listening, mapping and creating a short individual or collaborative sonic study. The programme can be adapted to beginners, students or experienced artists.

What we do

  • Introduce plants, electrodes and basic signal capture
  • Observe variation and discuss the limits of interpretation
  • Route signals into accessible sound-making tools
  • Experiment with pitch, rhythm, timbre and modulation mappings
  • Present and discuss a short sound experiment

What participants learn

  • How to set up a basic plant-signal listening workflow
  • How to connect sensed values to controllable sound parameters
  • How different mappings change the sonic result
  • How to document and communicate a creative experiment

2-Day Intensive

  • Day 1 — theory, plant bioelectricity, sensors, signal acquisition and mapping principles
  • Day 2 — hands-on connections, listening experiments, sound design and a short group presentation

5-Day Programme

  • Day 1 — plant signals and creative technology
  • Day 2 — electrodes, capture and observation
  • Day 3 — translating signals into sound parameters
  • Day 4 — individual and collaborative experiments
  • Day 5 — presentations, documentation and reflection

Informed by the Goethe-Institut workshop and CES Plant Music series (14 hours across four sessions). Shared devices are available; optional individual take-home devices can be included in a custom quote. No coding required.

Recorded Works

Plant Music

PLANTLOVERS is an evolving artistic and musical library of plant bioelectric signals, collected through recordings, field explorations, workshops and site-specific projects across different parts of the world.

Each work connects a particular plant, place and moment. Bioelectric sensing and modular synthesis transform measured activity into sound, shaped by artistic interpretation.
