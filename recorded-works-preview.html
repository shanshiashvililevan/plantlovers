PLANTLOVERS / LEVAN SHANSHIASHVILIDESIGN PREVIEW — NOT PUBLISHED
Recorded Works / Five Releases

A Living Archive
of Plant Signals

PLANTLOVERS is an evolving artistic and musical archive of plant bioelectric signals, collected through field explorations, recordings, workshops and site-specific projects in different parts of the world.

Each work documents an encounter with a particular plant, place and moment. Measured signals are translated into sound through artistic interpretation, modular synthesis and electronic composition.

More than a catalogue of releases, this is an expanding library of listening, observation and collaboration.
