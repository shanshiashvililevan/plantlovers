# PLANTLOVERS — Technical Rider (Working Version)

**Programmes:** Listening to Plants (lecture + live demonstration); Plant Signals to Sound (2-day intensive or 5-day workshop). Technical requirements to be confirmed with each host.

## Venue provides
- Video projector and projection screen, with appropriate HDMI connection or adaptor.
- Professional, appropriately sized stereo PA system, tested and operational before participants enter.
- Audio mixing console with at least two free line-level channels (stereo pair), suitable cabling and DI boxes where needed. An analogue mixer is welcome but not mandatory if a suitable professional mixer is provided.
- On-site sound engineer for setup, line check, soundcheck and the live demonstration. For multi-day workshops, technical support must be available when audio routing is in use.
- Stable mains power, safely distributed power outlets and extension cables; a stable table for the artist's instruments and laptop.
- Quiet, appropriately lit presentation/workshop space, seating and tables suitable for the confirmed group size; plants and safe working surfaces to be agreed.
- Reliable access to the space for setup and soundcheck **before** the advertised start time; no public admission until audio and projection have been tested.

## PLANTLOVERS provides / confirms
- Plant-signal devices and artist instruments available for shared demonstrations, subject to the agreed programme.
- Final input list, connections, computer/video outputs, power requirements and stage/table layout after venue confirmation.
- Optional additional individual devices for participants are **not included** and require a separate approved equipment budget.

## Production and expenses
- Artist fee is negotiated individually after the host shares its programme budget.
- Travel, accommodation, local transportation, additional equipment, technical rental and other approved production expenses are **separate** from the artist fee and paid by the host.
- Artist fee payment: **50% on contract signature and 50% before the programme starts**. Advance-booked travel/equipment costs must be paid or booked separately in sufficient time.
- A written contract should confirm the programme scope, schedule, cancellation terms, invoicing/tax treatment and responsibilities.
